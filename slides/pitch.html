1 · Choosing the problem

We picked a benchmark the frontier hasn't solved yet

AutomationBench (Zapier): real business workflows across CRM, email, Sheets and Slack, graded on the final app state.

  • Started below 10%. April 2026 paper: "even the best frontier models currently score below 10%."
  • Best today: ~50%. Claude Opus 5 still fails half the tasks.
  • ~50 points of headroom, unlike saturated benchmarks.
  • Our frontier bar: Kimi K3, 46.7%, rerun by us on held-out tasks.

3 · Lever ① · Holo-style harness

Same model, better habits

1 · Operating procedure in the system prompt

Adapted from Holo4's prompt: find governing policies first, write a checklist of every change, look tools up instead of guessing, change only what was asked.

2 · Verify before finishing

The first time the agent tries to stop, we interrupt once and ask it to check its work against the checklist.

v1 → v2 · tuned on calibration failures only

Resolve internal IDs to what the API expects · name every person, ID, amount, date in messages · lighter final check that only looks for gaps.

7 · Lessons learned

What we'd tell the next team

1 · Combine, don't bet on one trick

No single system wins everywhere. Perfect picks among the five reach 70%; the best one alone gets 58%.

2 · Tune the harness on failures

Our first harness lowered the score (−6.7). Rules written from calibration failures recovered it.

3 · Distill cheaply, mind coverage

$11 and 5 minutes gave 2–5×. The limit is demos: ~40% of task types had none.

4 · LLM judges can overfit

A trajectory judge looked useful on calibration (AUC 0.66), then fell below chance on test (0.42).

5 · Small screens mislead

DeepSeek scored 66.7% on a 30-task screen and 56.7% on held-out test. Keep a clean test set.

6 · One platform, fast loops

Inference, embeddings and LoRA training all on Fireworks: idea → fine-tuned model → eval in an afternoon.
Next:  collect demos for the uncovered 40% of tasks, and route on early-run signals to close the gap from 58% toward 70%.
